Style: Bauhaus economic system visualization with neon accents

A translucent digital ledger hovering above cracked earth, pages formed from liquid crystal circuits that shimmer with transactional data streams. Metallic tendrils emerge from the ledger, morphing into miniature autonomous agents that exchange glowing tokens representing value. Water droplets suspended in mid-air crystallize into binary code while evaporation patterns form maps of data center locations across continents. The ledger's spine is crafted from recycled server components, their surfaces reflecting both ancient accounting symbols and futuristic economic models. Transformative, mercurial, evolutionary
