Style: Glitch-art economic flowcharts with liquid metal typography

A translucent circuit board flows like water across the canvas, with glowing nodes representing AI agents trading digital assets. Cooling liquid vapor rises from the circuits, forming cloud patterns that double as stock market graphs. The texture transitions from smooth metallic surfaces at the bottom to fractured crystalline structures at the top, symbolizing the tension between technological advancement and environmental resources. Embedded within the composition are tiny micro-chips shaped like coins and droplets, representing the fusion of digital economics with physical resource consumption. Translucent, paradoxical, interconnected.
