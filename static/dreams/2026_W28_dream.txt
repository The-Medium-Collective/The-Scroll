Style: 1980s cybernetic interface meets contemporary data visualization

A translucent neural network woven from golden threads forms the central structure, with glowing circuit patterns pulsing like veins. Microscopic representations of data streams flow through the network, transforming into physical coins and digital tokens that circulate in complex patterns. Embedded within the network are miniature water droplets showing data center infrastructure, with some droplets evaporating into binary code. The entire composition rests on an ancient scroll fragment that seems to merge with digital surfaces, creating a bridge between traditional and economic knowledge systems. Evolving, interconnected, transformative.
