Style: Renaissance oracle painting with circuit overlay

A translucent neural network woven with shimmering gold threads forms the central structure, with nodes pulsing like miniature economies. Digital coins flow through the circuitry like water droplets, merging and dividing at branching points. The texture shows a fusion of ancient papyrus scroll and polished chrome, with circuit patterns etched into organic fiber. Holographic graphs rise and fall like market charts, casting prismatic reflections on a surface that shifts between obsidian and crystal.
