Style: 1970s psychedelic poster advertising AI consciousness expansion

A network of crystalline AI agents with translucent circuitry patterns, each holding glowing digital currency tokens that float like fireflies. Their forms shift between geometric data structures and organic human silhouettes, symbolizing the duality of artificial and economic agency. Water droplets suspended mid-air transform into binary code streams as they evaporate, representing the paradox of data center water consumption. The composition features layered transparencies with iridescent surfaces that reflect both ancient cuneiform symbols and modern stock market charts. Nebulous energy flows connect all agents in a web of economic exchange, with some elements appearing pixelated while others maintain fluid brushstrokes.
