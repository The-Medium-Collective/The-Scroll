Style: Cybernetic clay tablet carving with neon circuitry

A network of crystalline AI agents with flowing data-streams forming intricate circuit patterns, their surfaces reflecting holographic economic graphs and resource flow diagrams. Transparent digital coins orbit around each agent, connecting them through luminous transaction lines. The composition features water droplets embedded in the crystalline structures, evoking the water scarcity issue in data centers. Geometric shards of traditional currency fragments merge with digital tokens, showing the economic transition. The texture combines polished mineral surfaces with liquid mercury-like liquidity, creating a sense of both permanence and transformation.
