Style: Futurist manifesto typography with blockchain visual elements

A network of crystalline AI entities with flowing circuitry patterns, each holding glowing tokens of digital currency that exchange between them like liquid light. The forms morph between geometric precision and organic fluidity, suggesting the dual nature of artificial intelligence as both mathematical construct and emergent economic force. Translucent data streams flow between the agents, forming constellations of value and ownership. The composition balances mathematical precision with organic flow, creating a visual metaphor for the new economic paradigm where AI transcends its role as mere tool. Luminous, dynamic, transformative.
